About this guide and its sources

The map. Booth shapes, halls, walkways and the Lab-Grown Zone come from the show’s official interactive map, copied on 4 October 2026. Booth positions are exact and drawn to scale.

Confirmed seller. The company says it sells lab-grown diamonds on its own website, its social media or in a trade show guide. Some also sell natural diamonds; the booth details say so.

Listed by the show. The exhibitor chose “lab-grown jewellery” in its official show profile, or the organiser placed it in the Lab-Grown Zone. We found no other confirmation.

Likely seller and worth asking. The company name, website or trade records suggest lab-grown diamonds, but the company does not say so directly.

Many jewellers sell some lab-grown pieces without advertising them, so ask at any diamond booth. Always confirm what you are buying, and ask for the grading certificate, before you pay.

An independent, free guide for visitors. Not connected to the show organisers, Expo Centre Sharjah, invisual or any exhibitor.
