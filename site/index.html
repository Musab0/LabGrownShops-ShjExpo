How this guide was made

Floor plan. Booth shapes, halls, walkways and the Lab Grown zone come from the official interactive map’s data, fetched on 4 October 2026. Positions are exact and drawn to scale.

Verified. The company says it sells lab-grown diamonds on its own website, its social pages or a trade show guide. Some sell natural diamonds too; the entry says so.

Listed by the show. The exhibitor ticked “lab-grown jewellery” in its show profile, or the organiser placed it in the Lab Grown zone, without a separate confirmation.

Likely and ask. Name, website or trade records point to lab-grown without a direct statement.

Many jewellers carry lab-grown pieces without advertising them, so ask at any diamond booth. Confirm details at the booth before you buy.
